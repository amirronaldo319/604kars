The job

Everything between your driveway and the deposit.

Most owners quit Turo over the admin, not the economics. This is the admin.

LISTING

Listing and pricing

Photography, description, trip settings, and pricing that moves with Vancouver demand instead of sitting at one flat rate all year.

GUESTS

Screening and messaging

Every inquiry answered, every guest vetted before approval. Questions at 11pm are ours, not yours.

HANDOFF

Delivery and pickup

Meeting guests, walkarounds, photo documentation at both ends, and airport runs when the trip calls for it.

TURNOVER

Cleaning and refuelling

Interior and exterior between trips, fuel or charge topped up, so the next guest sees the same car the last one did.

UPKEEP

Maintenance tracking

Oil, tires, seasonal changeover and service intervals tracked against real mileage, with your approval before any spend.

CLAIMS

Damage and disputes

Documentation, filing and follow-through with Turo when something goes wrong — the part most owners dread.

One booking, start to finish

What happens while
you do nothing.

01 / INQUIRY

A guest asks

The request lands in our inbox. We answer fast — response time is one of the biggest levers on how often your car gets picked.

02 / SCREENING

We vet them

Trip history, ratings, age, and reason for the trip. If something reads wrong we decline it. You never see the ones we turn away.

03 / HANDOFF

Keys change hands

We meet the guest, walk the car, and photograph every panel with timestamps. That evidence is what wins claims later.

04 / TRIP

They drive

We’re on call for extensions, lockouts and roadside issues for the whole trip. Your phone stays quiet.

05 / RETURN

The car comes back

Second walkaround, same angles, fuel and mileage logged. Any new damage is filed the same day, not the next week.

06 / RESET

Cleaned and topped up

Interior, exterior, fuel or charge. The car goes back on the calendar ready — not "ready once I get to it".

07 / PAYOUT

You get paid

Turo pays out, we take our share, and you get a statement showing every trip, every expense and every dollar.

The split

One number.
No line items.

We take a share of what your car earns, so we only do well when it does. There’s no monthly fee, no onboarding charge, and nothing to pay in a month your car sits still.

20%of host earnings — everything below included

  • Listing, pricing and calendar management
  • Guest screening, messaging and support during trips
  • Handoffs, inspections, cleaning and refuelling
  • Claims filed and followed through on your behalf
  • Not included: insurance, registration and mechanical repairs stay with you as the owner
