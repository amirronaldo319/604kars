Set The Right Expectations

Co-hosting isn't completely passive.

We make vehicle management easier, but owning a rental vehicle still comes with real responsibilities.

  • Vehicles accumulate mileage
  • Maintenance is required
  • Demand fluctuates seasonally
  • Revenue varies month to month
  • Vehicles may experience normal wear
  • Bookings aren't guaranteed
  • Operating expenses affect profitability

Our job is to manage the operational complexity — not to promise guaranteed income.

Trip Management System

Every trip runs on the same clock.

Consistency is what makes hands-off ownership possible. Every booking moves through the same three checkpoints.

T–24H

Before the trip

  • Confirm booking & guest details
  • Confirm vehicle readiness
  • Send pickup instructions
T–1H

Right before pickup

  • Confirm vehicle access
  • Confirm vehicle location
  • Verify guest has instructions
T+2H

After expected return

  • Confirm the vehicle has been returned
  • Check vehicle status
  • Begin post-trip inspection

Good Fit

604 Kars may be a good fit if...

  • Your car sits unused regularly
  • You have a second vehicle
  • You travel frequently
  • You work from home
  • You want income from an asset you already own
  • You don't want to manage guests yourself
  • You're interested in building a small fleet over time

Not A Fit — Yet

It may not be right if...

  • You need your car every day
  • Your vehicle is unreliable or unsafe to drive
  • You don't want additional mileage on it
  • Your vehicle isn't eligible for the platform
  • You expect guaranteed income
