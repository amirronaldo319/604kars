Privacy Policy

This is a template privacy policy drafted for 604 Kars' current form (name, email, phone, location) and general BC PIPA (Personal Information Protection Act) obligations. It is not a substitute for legal advice — have it reviewed by a qualified professional before relying on it for compliance, especially before running paid advertising.

Last updated: August 9, 2026

1. Who we are

604 Kars ("we," "us," "our") provides Turo co-hosting and vehicle management services in Vancouver, North Vancouver, Burnaby, and Richmond, British Columbia. You can reach us at 604kars@gmail.com or 236-788-8944.

2. Information we collect

When you submit our contact form or book a consultation, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • General location (city)

We do not collect payment information, government ID numbers, or vehicle registration details through this website. Any such information is exchanged separately once we begin working together, under your management agreement.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and discuss your vehicle
  • Prepare a personalized earnings estimate
  • Schedule a consultation
  • Communicate with you about our services

4. How we share your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share information with service providers who help us operate (such as email or scheduling tools) solely to deliver the services above, and only to the extent necessary.

5. Your rights under BC PIPA

Under British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act, you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request corrections, and withdraw consent for us to contact you. To exercise these rights, email 604kars@gmail.com.

6. Data retention

We retain contact information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry or, if you become a client, for the duration of our management agreement plus any period required by law.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information you share with us, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email 604kars@gmail.com or call 236-788-8944.
